How the agents operate — the loop

A loop that runs whether or not anyone is watching.

  1. 1

    Sense

    Each agent watches the feeds behind its mandates — continuously.

  2. 2

    Find

    When reality drifts, it raises a finding and traces the impact up to the intent it threatens.

  3. 3

    Decide

    The finding demands a human call — Accept, Act, Park, or Dismiss. Unanswered, it escalates.

  4. 4

    Act

    A recovery target to watch, a fix broken into tasks, or a deliberate park.

  5. 5

    Close

    Nothing is “done” until the number is back — and every decision lands in the ledger with a verdict. Then back to sensing.

The decision — four ways to answer

The agent brings the finding. The human owns the call.

Accept

It's real — a recovery target is set and the agent watches until the number is back.

Act

Open a fix, broken into tasks, straight into the build loop.

Park

Known issue, parked with a re-alert rule — it comes back louder if it worsens.

Dismiss

Not real — a reason is required, and the reason tunes the agent.
